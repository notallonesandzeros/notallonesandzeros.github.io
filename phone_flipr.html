About This Project

A comprehensive inventory management system designed for ecommerce sellers who need to manage their stock and sales across multiple platforms. Keep your eBay store and Shopify store perfectly synchronized from one unified dashboard.

No more updating items in two places. Manage your entire product catalog, inventory levels, and orders from this single platform while everything stays automatically synchronized across your selling channels.

Key Features

Custom Tables

Create your own custom tables. Link them together with linked records. Create formula columns for automatic calculations, text, dates, numbers, currencies, unique id's and more.

Unified Inventory Management

Track individual items with serial numbers, conditions, and variants. Create and manage your product catalog with smart SKU generation and eBay-compatible condition mappings.

Automated Workflows

Gmail integration for order notifications, automated listing creation, webhook processing for real-time updates, and business policy synchronization.

Perfect For

  • Electronics Resellers - Ideal for phone and device flippers managing inventory across multiple channels
  • Multi-channel Sellers - Perfect for businesses selling on both eBay and Shopify
  • Inventory-Heavy Businesses - Great for tracking individual items with serial numbers and conditions
  • Automation-Focused Sellers - Designed for users who want automated listing and order processing

Work in Progress: This project is currently under active development. The core inventory management and platform synchronization features are being refined based on real-world usage patterns from ecommerce sellers.
